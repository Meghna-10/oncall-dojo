AI models

Three Nemotron models, three distinct jobs

Nemotron 3 NanoThe stressed manager

Plays your manager and support agent. Called every ~40 s, so it must be fast: replies in about 2 s with reasoning off.

Nemotron 3 SuperThe coach & risk analyst

Knows the hidden root cause and nudges you where to look. Also powers the Change Risk Radar's pre-mortem.

Nemotron 3 UltraThe reviewer

Reasons over your whole timeline (reasoning on) and writes a blameless post-incident review.
